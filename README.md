# 🥇 GoldScope AI
### Intelligent Gold Market Analysis & Contract Intelligence Platform

**Understand the market. Detect unusual behaviour. Make data-driven decisions.**

GoldScope AI is an intelligent financial analytics platform designed to analyse gold futures contracts, identify unusual price movements, compare contract relationships and transform complex market data into understandable insights.

The platform aims to bring gold market analysis, anomaly detection, historical backtesting and financial intelligence together in one interactive dashboard.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

Gold markets involve multiple futures contracts with different specifications, expiry dates and trading activity. Understanding how these contracts relate to one another can be challenging, especially when their prices behave unusually.

GoldScope AI addresses this challenge by providing a centralized platform for analysing gold contract relationships, detecting abnormal market behaviour and studying historical trends.

Rather than simply displaying gold prices, the platform focuses on explaining what is happening in the market and helping users investigate potential pricing anomalies.

## 🧠 AI & Analytical Approach

GoldScope AI is designed around quantitative financial analysis and machine learning.

Potential analytical capabilities include:

- **Anomaly Detection:** Identifying unusual movements in gold contract relationships.
- **Regression Analysis:** Studying relationships between different contracts.
- **Mean Reversion Analysis:** Evaluating whether historical price differences tend to return towards their typical levels.
- **Market Regime Detection:** Identifying changes in historical market behaviour.
- **Explainable Insights:** Translating analytical results into understandable research observations.

The current statistical signal engine should not be confused with a trained machine learning model. Additional ML capabilities can be integrated and evaluated as the platform develops.

## 📊 How It Works

1. **Data Collection:** Obtain historical gold futures contract data.
2. **Data Processing:** Organize and prepare contract price information.
3. **Contract Comparison:** Analyse relationships between selected contracts.
4. **Signal Detection:** Identify unusual price differences using statistical methods.
5. **Visualization:** Present findings through interactive charts and dashboards.
6. **Backtesting:** Evaluate historical behaviour using defined assumptions.

## 💡 What Makes GoldScope AI Different?

GoldScope AI aims to move beyond traditional price-tracking dashboards.

Its focus is on understanding relationships between gold contracts, identifying unusual market behaviour and making complex financial analysis easier to interpret.

The long-term vision is to evolve into a comprehensive gold market intelligence platform combining quantitative analysis, explainable machine learning, scenario simulation and financial risk intelligence.
